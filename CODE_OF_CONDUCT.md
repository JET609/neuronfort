# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want NeuronFort to be a welcoming, respectful place for learners and practitioners of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, constructive, and open to feedback.
- Focus discussions on defensive security, education, and responsible practice.
- Respect privacy, consent, and applicable laws.
- Credit sources and contributors accurately.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Encouraging unauthorized access, destructive activity, or harmful misuse.
- Spam, plagiarism, or deliberately misleading content.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this code. Report concerns privately through the contact route described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
